About

Obsessed with the details others overlook.

Hi! I'm Carlos a Computer Science student at Hunter College finishing up in 2027. I write mostly Python, C++, and Swift recently got into OpenGL and graphics programming which I didn't expect to enjoy as much as I do.

I enjoy building projects that feel useful. Someone close to me works at a cafe, and watching them nativagate the day got me thinking about what the data behind a coffee shop actually looks like — so I built a dashboard around it. A class project on 3D vectors turned into a real-time OpenGL renderer because I wanted to see what it could actually become. That's usually how it goes for me. Graduated high school early to get a head start.

When I'm not coding I'm at a cafe, watching sports, hanging around my favorite people or thinking through the next idea.
